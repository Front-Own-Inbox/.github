## First Look at Front

Front is for people who administer a shared inbox they own and want one named front desk, not mixed untitled queues. It gives you an own inbox, a teammate view, and a short path back to a shared queue you already keep. It does not replace the rest of your PC.

Work stays on mail you administer. Extra queues wait for their own title.

## Inside Front

Front is a local Windows inbox workspace with a named front desk, a short shared queue, and a clear last handoff. It does not try to replace the rest of the PC. It sits beside an inbox you already administer and holds only the queue that is open now.
The layout stays small on purpose: one desk, one queue, one path back. Extra queues wait for their own title. Front starts with a local profile and does not need a complex account system.
